# Identity, illustration and imagery

## Mark

Use `porto-mark-handdrawn.png` as the approved broad P. Keep it square, preserve transparent margins and clear space equal to the red port diameter on every side. Minimum digital size: 24 px. Do not stretch, recolour the red port, add a shadow, introduce cyan glitches or substitute a generic P. The primary wordmark is supplied as editable text until a verified vector wordmark export is approved. Proposed partner lockups must be labelled proposed.

## Asset registry

| Asset | Use | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pillar-broken.png` | Problem slides | Right third, leave left 65% clear. |
| `pillar-centre.png` | Porto / ownership slides | Single intact pillar, never use for problem. |
| `arch-right.png`, `plinth-left.png` | Cover and dense-content anchors | Crop only at natural architectural edges. |
| `steps-top-right.png`, `rails-minimal-study.png` | Milestones and closing | Restrained, never literal process diagrams. |
| `player-phone-modern.png` | Deck product treatment | Preserve phone geometry. |
| `player-responsive.svg` | Website player | Preserve 154:332 ratio. |

Photography remains colour. A portrait gets a paper or charcoal label, never a duotone overlay. Architectural art is a raster background layer with a separate contrast scrim if copy needs it. Circuit traces are 1 px, sparse, with endpoint rings and no decorative network mesh.
