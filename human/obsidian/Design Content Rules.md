# Voice and content

Direct, confident and concise. Speak to artists, labels and investors without hype. Avoid `decentralized`, `web3`, `blockchain`, `crypto` and `NFT` in hero or above-the-fold marketing.

| Use | Suitable | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | `Distribution should belong to the people who make the music.` | `A revolutionary Web3 music future` |
| Economics | `70% to rights holders, split by declared rights.` | `Artists always receive 70%.` |
| Operations | `Operators earn for serving and attesting eligible activity.` | `Anyone earns 25% by joining.` |
| Settlement | `Beta uses daily settlement epochs.` | `Every stream settles instantly.` |

Public examples use publicly supportable language and generic contacts. Private valuations, biography detail and commercial material belong in restricted files only. No em dashes.
