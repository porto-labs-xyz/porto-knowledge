# Document templates

| Template | Minimum pages | Key behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-pager | 1 | Statement, three proof points, CTA and source line. |
| Executive brief | 2 | Executive summary plus detail continuation. |
| Report | 4 | Title, contents, section opener, table/chart, running header and page number. |
| Litepaper / whitepaper | 6 | Multi-level headings, figure/table captions, citations, appendix. |
| Case study | 2 | Context, method, result, evidence and quote. |
| Proposal | 3 | Scope, approach, timeline and commercial placeholder. |
| Press release | 2 | Date line, headline, body, boilerplate and contact property. |
| Letterhead | 1 | Mark, address property, date, salutation and signature property. |
| Appendix | 2 | Tables, definitions, sources and continuation footer. |

Editorial Light is proposed. Use it for content-rich reports with paper background, charcoal type, coral section markers and subtle architectural fragments only on section openers. Legal Plain uses black Times New Roman, 11 pt body, restrained 14 pt headings and page number. It contains neutral dummy content only and does not alter legal wording.
