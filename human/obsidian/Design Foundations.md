# Foundations

## Modes and frames

| Mode | Use | Frame and grid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing Dark | Deck, landing, campaign | 16:9: 1920×1080, 12 columns, 64 outer margin, 24 gutter. Social: preserve at least 7% safe area. |
| Editorial Light, proposed | Reports, case studies, press materials | A4 210×297 mm or US Letter 8.5×11 in, 12 mm inner, 15 mm outer, 6 columns. |
| Legal Plain | Neutral legal presentation only | A4 or US Letter, 25 mm margins, one column, 11 pt Times New Roman. |

Use Auto Layout for all reusable text stacks, controls, cards, lists, rows and tables. Use constraints for frame-level architectural art. Do not rasterise text, charts, tables, logos or layouts.

## Type styles

Deck: Display 56/60 Avenir Next Demi Bold, H1 40/46, H2 28/34, Body 16/25, Caption 11/16. Use IBM Plex Mono for eyebrow 12/19 uppercase, data labels 12/16 and percentage figures. Website retains DM Sans for display/body, with IBM Plex Mono used for hero, navigation, labels, buttons and percentage figures. Letter-spacing is tight only for display, never body.

## Surface language

Near-black is warm charcoal, never blue-black. Use paper for primary text, muted for secondary. Coral is an accent, not a body-text substitute. Dense content may use the Glass/Card component: `glass`, 1 px `line`, 14 px radius, subtle inner highlight. Economics bars and donut labels are open compositions, with no panel or pill.

Architectural texture is restrained: fine warm contour, spaced diagonal hatching, limited pigment flecks and construction rings. Texture layers sit behind data geometry and never encode data. Avoid neon, cyberpunk, cartoons, heavy wobble, dense crosshatching and distorted geometry.

## Accessibility and export

Use a 4.5:1 text contrast target. Paper on charcoal and ink on paper are default body combinations. Do not use colour alone for an allocation: include name, value and legend. Figma prototype focus: 2 px coral outline, 6 px offset. Supply alt text with each export. Export slide/master previews at 2× PNG and artwork as original PNG/SVG; never export the primary content as a flattened screenshot.
