# Presentation templates, 16:9

All masters use 1920×1080, Marketing Dark mode, a 12-column grid and 64 px safe margin. Place copy in editable Auto Layout stacks; the art layer is constrained independently.

| Master | Required composition |
| --- | --- |
| `Deck/Cover` | Broad P, title, short positioning, right-side arch or pillar. |
| `Deck/Problem` | Broken-pillar background, `01 / The Problem`, evidence panel. |
| `Deck/Porto` | Intact pillar, `02 / Porto`, Artist/Distributor Venn, Porto centred in overlap. |
| `Deck/Ownership` | Three-layer infrastructure explanation and source note. |
| `Deck/Product` | Player at natural deck treatment, never compressed. |
| `Deck/Comparison` | Two exact economics bars, open background. |
| `Deck/Economics` | Traditional and Porto allocation donuts, open labels. |
| `Deck/Market` | Chart, explanatory panel and source notes. |
| `Deck/Timeline` | Milestone row with status qualification. |
| `Deck/Milestones` | Low and high density variants, optional glass panel. |
| `Deck/Team` | Colour portraits and role text. |
| `Deck/Partners` | Partner strip, credentials where approved. |
| `Deck/Commercial-context` | Restricted master, red `PRIVATE` label and replaceable contact properties. |
| `Deck/Closing` | Exact line: `Porto puts distribution in the hands of the people who make the music.` |

Make a cover, comparison, market, team and closing example with realistic copy before release. The `Commercial-context` master stays out of public exports and this package's general examples.
