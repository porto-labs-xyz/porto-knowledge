# Representative end-to-end briefs

## Deck, public investor introduction

Audience: early-stage music infrastructure investors. Purpose: explain why distribution ownership matters. Channel: 16:9 deck. Mode: Marketing Dark. Privacy: public-safe. Content source: whitepaper and PIP-5 only for specific settlement claims. Output: PDF and editable Figma presentation. Required pages: cover, problem, Porto Venn, product, exact economics comparison, 70/25/5 allocation, source slide and closing. Accessibility: source labels and tabular data alternative.

## Report, operator briefing

Audience: prospective infrastructure operators. Purpose: explain the operational role without overstating Beta. Channel: A4 report. Mode: Editorial Light, proposed. Privacy: public-safe. Content source: PIP-5/PIP-8. Output: 6-page PDF and editable Figma document. Requirement: state that Beta has a default daily settlement epoch, distinguish accrual from settlement and explain that the operator allocation follows eligible service/attestation under the relevant configuration.

## Campaign, artist ownership

Audience: independent artists and labels. Purpose: awareness campaign. Channel: 4:5 carousel plus 9:16 story. Mode: Marketing Dark. Privacy: public-safe. Content source: landing voice and verified whitepaper claims. Output: editable Figma campaign plus PNG exports. Core line: `Distribution should belong to the people who make the music.` Avoid crypto language.
