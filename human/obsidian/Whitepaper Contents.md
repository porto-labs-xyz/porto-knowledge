# Porto Whitepaper

This repository contains the canonical Porto whitepaper: **"Porto: A Network-Owned Infrastructure Layer for Music Distribution."**

Porto is decentralized streaming infrastructure that replaces platform-owned delivery and settlement with a network run by artists, labels, publishers, and independent node operators. Every play is logged on Porto Chain, a purpose-built blockchain, and settled automatically on-chain , replacing a 90–180 day intermediary payout chain with same-epoch, auditable settlement.

## Contents

- [`porto-whitepaper.md`](./porto-whitepaper.md) , the full paper, covering:
  1. Introduction , what Porto is and isn't
  2. The Problem , opacity, delay, and margin extraction in current streaming royalties
  3. The Porto Network , system overview and participant roles
  4. Porto Chain , the app-chain architecture
  5. PRT , the token model
  6. Streaming and stream accounting
  7. Payouts , the settlement model
  8. Governance
  9. Phased rollout, from Beta to permissionless mainnet
  10. Market position
  11. Team
  12. Conclusion

Normative technical specifications, written in an EIP/RFC-style format, live separately in the Porto Improvement Proposal (PIP) series (`../pips/`) and are referenced throughout the paper.
